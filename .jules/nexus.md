## 2023-10-31 — Personal Defense Digest
**Product understood as:** An AI-powered sovereign defense platform against vishing, smishing, and telecom fraud.
**Derivation reasoning:** We store detailed data about threats, spam blocks, and calculate a "safety score" for users over time (`ScoreHistory`), but citizens only see a global "Command Center" dashboard or individual alerts. There is no unified place for a user to see their personal protection metrics. Users who are protected by the app need to see the value it provides to them personally.
**Feature built:** Added a "My Defense Digest" view for citizens that summarizes their current safety score, historical score trend, and a breakdown of threats avoided.
**User impact:** Citizens can now track their personal cybersecurity posture and see exactly how many scams the system has deflected for them.
**Next logical feature:** Community/Local Threat Warnings (notifying users if a nearby area or known contact was compromised).
