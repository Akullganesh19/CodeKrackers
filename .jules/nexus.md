## 2024-05-18 — Personal Safety Profile
**Product understood as:** A comprehensive Vishing and Smishing defense platform that monitors, detects, and acts on real-time threats while tracking user safety metrics.
**Derivation reasoning:** This product has user entities containing `safety_score`, `scams_avoided`, and `ScoreHistory` data. Users use the system and report threats, but they can never see their own safety standing. Therefore users obviously need a Personal Safety Profile dashboard — because they need a feedback loop to track their security posture and understand the platform's value. It doesn't exist because the initial focus was heavily on macro-level analytics and threat detection for admins, rather than individual citizen feedback. I'm building it because giving users visibility into their safety metrics increases engagement, trust, and proactive security behaviors.
**Feature built:** Added a `GET /me/safety` backend endpoint and a `My Safety Profile` frontend page for users to view their safety score, scams avoided, reported threats, and historical score trend.
**User impact:** Users can now view their personal safety profile, see how many scams they have avoided, monitor their safety score, and track their security posture over time.
**Next logical feature:** Gamification and achievements based on `scams_avoided` or maintaining high safety scores over consecutive days to further encourage secure behavior.
