## 2024-05-24 — Predictive SMS Fetching
**Product understood as:** Cybersecurity platform for scanning SMS/calls.
**Prediction invented:** Predictive fetching of SMS scan results before user hits "Analyze".
**Data used:** The contents of the SMS being pasted into the `sms-scanner` textarea.
**Impact:** Users will experience instantaneous analysis results because the inference request happens in the background.
**Next opportunity:** Route-prefetching for dashboards and analytics pages.
