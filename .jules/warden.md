## 2026-10-28 — [Structural PII Redaction in Logs]
**Data traced:** PII (Emails, Phone Numbers) and Secrets (passwords, tokens).
**Exposure found:** Logged in plaintext in various logs such as `logger.info("USER_CREATED email=%s role=%s", user.email, user.role.value)`, OTP failures, account locking events, and notification sends in `backend/api/users.py`, `backend/api/auth.py`, `backend/api/login.py`, `backend/services/notifier.py`, `backend/api/v1/endpoints/users.py`, etc.
**Fix:** Introduced structural PII and secret redaction at the root logging layer (`backend/core/logger.py`) by adding a custom `structlog` processor (`redact_sensitive_data`). Ensured `setup_logging()` is called at the top of `backend/main.py`. The redaction handles strings using regex and explicitly matching dictionary keys to fully redact secrets.
**Coverage confirmed:** Ran `python3 test_logger.py` to trigger structlog with kwargs and f-strings and verified the output format successfully obfuscates sensitive keys (e.g., `email`, `phone`, `password`, `secret`) and regex-matches PII strings in the event message.
**Still exposed elsewhere:** While active logs are mitigated structurally, static legacy log files (e.g., `logs/app_2026-05-08.log`) or `backend/data/feature_log.jsonl` may still contain historical PII. There could be other data deletion paths missing, or audits logging PII in DB.
