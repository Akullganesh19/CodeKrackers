## 2026-05-08 — PII Logging Exposure Closed
**Data traced:** Emails, phone numbers, and OTP codes.
**Exposure found:** Actively leaked in plaintext into application logs across the authentication, threat intel, user management, and spam shield services. Additionally, CSV threat exports included unredacted source numbers and raw message contents.
**Fix:** Introduced an irreversible central redaction filter (`PIIRedactionFilter` and `redact_structlog_processor`) into `backend/core/logger.py` that intercepts all `logging` and `structlog` messages, automatically masking emails (`j***@example.com`), phone numbers (`+12***90`), and OTPs before they reach standard out or log files. Additionally modified the CSV export endpoints to apply `_redact_text` globally on output fields.
**Coverage confirmed:** The standard library `logging` root logger filter and the `structlog` custom processors were both verified to operate safely on nested dictionaries and strings. The regexes accurately mask without dropping legitimate application context.
**Still exposed elsewhere:** Audit logs in `backend/models/audit.py` store `user_email` and raw `details` JSON objects. These might be queried or exported in plaintext by admin interfaces in the future, meaning the internal data model still physically stores PII even if the app logs are clean. `threat.raw_content` is also stored verbatim.
